Join us for this special **two tours in one day event** in the high elevation of Fort Needham Memorial Park!

We will meet at 7:30 **AM** and hang out to observe the birds around us for an hour or so. The tour will then **reconvene** at 7:00 **PM** to see what's happening as sunset approaches.

Meet at the Parking Lot at the North End of the Park. We will proceed to the Memorial and surrounding area to take stock of the birds occupying and passing through the area. 

This is a rain or shine event.
