Join a tour of an interesting portion of a **migratory hotspot Brier Island**. An unbelievable diversity of migratory birds gather at this special funneling point before embarking on their fall migration south.

We will explore Big Pond Cove and walk toward Western Light at **2:00 PM** and return to our starting point around 4:00 PM. 

Bird banding will be taking place on the island during this time, which can be observed by curious and respectful visitors **in the morning** before or after the tour. It will surely be worth the trip (there is also the whale sideshow to enjoy while you're there). 

We'll meet at the Big Pond Cove Parking lot and take it from there. Please contact tours@haliflocks.ca or message us on Instagram if you are interested in observing the bird banding station in the mornings.
