Join us for this special tour of the internationally renowned **birding hotspot** Hartlen Point! We'll also be checking out MacCormacks Beach to practice some gull differentiation, if time allows

We will meet at 9 AM and navigate a somewhat secret path, spot a diversity of nesting and migratory birds, and find a beautiful beach look off. We'll see what we can find in this special part of HRM, and then make our way back to Shore Road to go visit MacCormacks Beach. MacCormacks Beach is a great place to practice gull identification.

Meet at the end of Shore Road, **right** **before** the Hartlen Point Forces Golf Course Parking Lot. 

Rain date for this event is August 16th.
